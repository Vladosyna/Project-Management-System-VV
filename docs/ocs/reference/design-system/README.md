Vionvision is a computer-vision and AIoT company (founded 2005): smart footfall sensors, the CBox edge terminal and the V-store BI platform for retail, malls, restaurants and car dealerships. The look is clean, white and technical: one strong blue, black type, square geometry and a 30° slash. Use this system for every report, proposal, presentation, one-pager and diagram.

## Voice and content

- Confident, precise, practical. Lead with the outcome for the client, then the mechanism. Quote real numbers with units and a source.
- Website headlines are Title Case statements: "Solutions With Richest Diversity & Adaptability", "Complete Conversion Funnel Analysis", "CV-empowered Loss Prevention". Use Title Case for slide titles and document H1s; sentence case for body, table captions and callouts.
- Write the company name as **Vionvision** in running text. VIONVISION in capitals exists only inside the logo.
- Product names keep their casing: V-store, CBox, TrueFlow, ReID.
- Documents and slides for UK/EU clients are in British English; APAC materials in Chinese or bilingual (English first line, Chinese second).
- Prefer "we" (Vionvision) and "you" (client). No emoji, no exclamation marks, no unsupported superlatives — "most accurate" only with a cited benchmark.
- Status words are plain and fixed: Done · In progress · At risk · Not started · Waiting on [owner].

## Logo

- Blue wordmark (`assets/Logos/vionvision-logo-blue.svg`, #0066FF) on `surface`, `surface-alt` and light photos. White wordmark (`assets/Logos/vionvision-logo-white.svg`) on `brand`, `navy` and dark photos.
- Clear space = the height of the letter O on every side. Minimum 25 mm wide in print, 100 px on screen.
- Top-left on document pages and cover/divider/closing slides. Never recolour, outline, stretch, rotate, shadow or lock it up with other marks; partner logos sit in a separate row, same height, separated by a `rule` line.

## Colour

- 60 / 30 / 10: `surface` white and `surface-alt` grey carry the page, `ink` black carries text, `brand` blue is the accent (logo, key numbers, one highlighted series, one block). A full-blue slide or band is allowed once per section as a divider.
- Blue has two roles: `brand` #0066FF (logo, buttons, blocks, slashes, chart series 1 — kept exact) and `brand-text` #005BE6 for every piece of blue text (links, page numbers, KPI figures, step numbers). Never set text in #0066FF on tints or grey.
- `navy` is the dark ground (website footer): cover alternatives, closing slides, dark bands. On navy, text is white and blue accent text is `on-navy-accent` #66A3FF, never #0066FF.
- Secondary text is `ink-muted` (#666666); tertiary text `ink-subtle` (#6E6E6E). The website grey #8D8D8D is a chart/fill colour (`chart-4`), never text.
- Tints `brand-tint`, `success-tint`, `warning-tint`, `neutral-tint` are for callouts, status fills and stage strips only — never for large page areas.
- `accent-orange` #FE9454 is a rare decorative fill. Never text; in charts on white use `chart-5` #D46B2A.
- Charts use `chart-1` … `chart-5` in that order: blue = the subject/Vionvision, navy = comparison, light blue = breakdown (always with data labels), grey = other/previous period, deep orange = single highlight. Gridlines in `rule`, labels in `ink-muted`, no 3D, no gradients.
- Office hex reference: Blue #0066FF (logo, fills) · Blue text #005BE6 · Blue text on navy #66A3FF · Navy #0C1B2B · Black #000000 · Grey text #666666 · Light grey #F0F1F6 · Table header #F2F2F2 · Lines #D9D9D9 · Blue tint #DCE9F5 · Green tint #E3F1E4 · Amber tint #FCEFD9 · Orange #FE9454 (decorative) · Chart orange #D46B2A.

## Typography

- Documents (Word, PDF, Excel): **Arial** for Latin, **Microsoft YaHei** for Chinese — they are on every company machine and match existing Vionvision deliverables. Styles `doc-title` 16 pt bold upper case, `h1` 13 pt bold numbered, `h2` 10.5 pt bold, `body` 10 pt, `small` 8–8.5 pt in `ink-muted`.
- Slides and web: `display` family — Alibaba PuHuiTi (free commercial licence from Alibaba) when installed, else Arial / Microsoft YaHei. Headlines Medium (500), never bold-black; body Regular. PowerPoint sizes: `slide-display` 44 pt, `slide-title` 28 pt, `slide-body` 16 pt (never under 14 pt), `eyebrow` 14 pt, `stat-figure` 48 pt, source line 10 pt.
- Left-align text. Max line length ~75 characters in documents. No underlines except links; no italics except the document subtitle.

## Layout and shape

- Square corners everywhere (`radius-0`): tables, callouts, cards, photos, colour blocks. Only buttons are pills (`radius-pill`); small chips use `radius-sm`.
- **The slash** is the brand shape: a parallelogram skewed by `slash-angle` (-30°). Use it as one large blue block bleeding off a cover edge, as the four-bar slash indicator, or as a small corner tick on a card. One slash element per page or slide.
- Section markers follow the website pattern "/ 01 Solution" (`eyebrow`).
- Spacing steps `space-1` … `space-16`. Documents: 20 mm margins, `space-6` above each numbered heading. Slides: 96 px inner margin, content on a 12-column grid.
- Shadows only on screen (`shadow-card`, `shadow-brand-glow`); printed documents use rules and tints, never shadows.

## Documents

- Every report/proposal follows `components/DocumentPage`: logo + runner header, title block with the Prepared for / Prepared by / Date / Status table, numbered sections, footer "Vionvision | Title — Status, Month YYYY · Page N".
- Structure: 1. Why (the problem in the client's terms) → what we propose → how it works → who does what → timeline → what we need from you → Next step (green callout).
- Use DataTable for any comparison of 3+ items, StatusFlow for stage processes, Callout for the one point per section the reader must not miss.
- File names: `Client_Document-Name_vN.pdf` (e.g. `PMI_Programme-Tracker_Proposal_v2.pdf`).

## Presentations

- 16:9, masters in `components/SlideLayouts`: cover (white + blue slash), divider (full blue), content (white), closing (navy).
- The slide title states the takeaway ("Conversion rose 18% after staff exclusion"), not the topic ("Results").
- One idea, one chart or diagram per slide; at most 6 bullets; every number has a source line in the footer.
- Product renders sit on `surface-alt`; photography is real retail environments (malls, stores, entrances, queues), people natural and in motion, never staged stock handshakes.

## Iconography

- Thin single-weight line icons (1.5 px at 24 px), square caps, in `ink` or `brand`; one icon set per document. No emoji, no filled multicolour icons, no clip-art.
- Diagrams: square boxes with 1px `rule` or `brand` outlines, arrows in `ink-muted`, the active or Vionvision-owned element filled `brand` with white text.

## Don'ts

- No gradients, glows on print, drop shadows on text, or rounded cards.
- No #0066FF text on tints, grey or `navy`; no orange text; no #8D8D8D text.
- Don't mix more than two tints on one page, or use colour as the only status cue.
- Don't redraw or retype the logo.
