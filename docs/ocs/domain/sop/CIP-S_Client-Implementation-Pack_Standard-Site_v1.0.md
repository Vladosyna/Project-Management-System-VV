**1. How Implementation Runs**

| **Step**                   | **You**                                                                  | **VionVision**                                                    | **Timing**                                                         |
|----------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **1 Site information**     | Fill the Site Information Card (Section 3), attach photos and floor plan | Review; ask follow-up questions in one list                       | At least 4 weeks before installation                               |
| **2 Design**               | Confirm sensor positions                                                 | Send the sensor position design                                   | VionVision: 1 working day for up to 10 stores · You: within 2 days |
| **3 Order and delivery**   | Issue the PO                                                             | Ship devices to site or to the local partner                      | Europe: 2+ weeks · East / South-East Asia: 1–2 weeks               |
| **4 Installation date**    | Confirm the date; arrange mall or landlord permits                       | Book the installer; check readiness with you 14 and 7 days before | 1–2 days                                                           |
| **5 Installation**         | Store manager present; sign the Installation Acceptance Form             | Install, connect and test the sensors                             | 2–4 hours on site                                                  |
| **6 Commissioning**        | —                                                                        | Remote configuration and accuracy check                           | 1–2 days, then 1 day for the accuracy check                        |
| **7 Handover and support** | Log in to the dashboard; report issues to your PM                        | Platform access, training, warranty support                       | Ongoing                                                            |

**2. Site Readiness — Required on Installation Day**

We confirm these with you 14 days and 7 days before installation. An installer is only sent once all applicable items are confirmed.

|-----|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| ☐   | Power available at each sensor position, or a PoE switch that will power the sensors.                                                       |
| ☐   | PoE switch powered on, with one free port per sensor.                                                                                       |
| ☐   | Internet access for the sensors, with outbound access to the VionVision EU Cloud Platform (eu.vion-cloud.com). Your PM sends the port list. |

**4. If the Site Is Not Ready on the Day**

| **Situation**                                 | **What it looks like**                                     | **What happens**                                                                                                                         |
|-----------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Store not ready or information wrong**      | Unfinished fit-out, different entrance, wrong address.     | We agree the fix with you by email. If information cannot be collected remotely, a paid survey visit may be arranged.                    |
| **No power, no PoE switch, or no free ports** | Discovered on arrival.                                     | We look for a solution on the spot with you. If none, installation stops and is rescheduled once the condition is met.                   |
| **IP details wrong or unusable**              | Gateway, IP range or ports do not match what was provided. | Resolved with your IT on the spot; escalated if needed. If unresolved, installation is rescheduled.                                      |
| **Firewall ports closed**                     | Sensors cannot reach the platform.                         | Sensors are installed and configured, but data will not flow until the ports are opened. Commissioning is completed remotely afterwards. |

Where a visit cannot go ahead because conditions confirmed in Section 2 were not met, a further visit may be chargeable under your contract.
