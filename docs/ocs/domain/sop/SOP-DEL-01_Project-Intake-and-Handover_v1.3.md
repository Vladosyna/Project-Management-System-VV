**1. Purpose and the Rule**

A project reaches Delivery once. Everything Sales learned in months of pre-sales — what was promised, to whom, on what terms, under which constraints — has to arrive with it, in writing, in one place. This SOP defines that place, what goes into it, and how Delivery accepts the project.

**2. Why This Exists**

Every item below reached Delivery late or not at all on recent projects. None of it was unknown to the company — it simply was not handed over.

| **Missing at handover (recent cases)**                                                       | **What it cost**                                                           |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| The client's KPI requirements file — it existed, but was not in Delivery's hands at kick-off | The KPI/accuracy report was delayed while the file was chased across teams |
| Editable point-design files, after the designer left the company                             | Re-surveyed sites had no owner for drawing updates                         |
| Which of two contracts governs a given site                                                  | Client handover documents were held until it was confirmed                 |
| Warranty terms                                                                               | Unknown to Delivery until the final completion report was drafted          |
| Site working window (no works after 10:00) and the MAC registration procedure                | Discovered in the first days of installation, compressing the schedule     |
| Network ownership and switch credentials held by a third party                               | Weeks of on-site diagnosis on a network VionVision did not control         |

**3. Scope**

**Full package:** new clients; new countries or cities for an existing client; any project with installation; rollouts; POCs; POC-to-rollout conversions. POCs are always Full — that is where accuracy bars and client expectations get set.

**Light package:** work on a site whose project folder has already been accepted (add-points, replacements, warranty extensions), and supply-only orders with no installation or configuration by VionVision. Light means only what is new or changed — the existing folder must still be current.

**Terms.** Handover package: the project folder plus the Handover Card. Handover Card: the one-document summary Sales fills for every project (Annex B). Site Information Card: the card the client fills from CIP-L or CIP-S (Annex A). Intake review: Delivery's check of the package. Responsibility transfer: the date from which the PM owns execution.

> • **Two folder standards, chosen per site.** Standard L — a full lifecycle folder, for large sites or any site with accompanying work (cabling, network remediation, integration, security review). Standard S — one flat folder of a few numbered files, for small standard sites: up to 20 sensors, mounting and connection only. A contract of 200 small stores is 200 Standard S sites.
>
> • **Who decides:** Sales proposes the standard with the early warning (step 0), so the right client pack goes out; the Delivery Director confirms it and records it at the intake review (Handover Card, Section 11).
>
> • **Project ID** is assigned by Sales when the folder is created and used in the OA application, email subjects, tickets and every project document.
>
> • **Site Information Card.** Filed in the site folder as SOP-DEL-02 sets out and attached to the Handover Card. The Handover Card carries only what the card does not: commercial terms, relationship-level contacts, communication rules, gaps and unwritten agreements.
>
> • **Restricted notes.** Everything in checklist group F goes into the restricted Internal Notes area defined in SOP-DEL-02 — never into the Handover Card or an open folder.

**9. Exceptions and Escalation**

**Conditional Start.** Only Hai Tao can authorise Delivery to start before acceptance — in writing, in DingTalk, naming the missing items and a deadline of no more than 5 working days. The missing items stay Sales' responsibility; delays and costs caused by them are attributed to the conditional start, not to Delivery.

**Disputes.** If Sales disagrees with a Returned decision, the Sales Lead escalates to Hai Tao, who decides within 2 working days. Work does not start while the dispute is open.

**10. After Transfer**

| **Sales keeps**                     | **Delivery owns**                       | **Joint**                                                       |
|-------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Contract, pricing, commercial terms | Project plan and schedule               | Steering meetings                                               |
| Commercial side of change orders    | Installers and partners                 | Escalations                                                     |
| Invoicing and payment collection    | Technical communication with the client | Scope changes (Sales negotiates, PM confirms feasibility first) |
| Executive-level relationship        | Site coordination and access            | Relationship-health updates                                     |
| New opportunities with the client   | Acceptance documents                    |                                                                 |

**No new promises without Delivery.** After transfer, Sales does not commit new scope, dates or features to the client until the PM has confirmed feasibility. Every agreed change is filed in the project folder and added to the Handover Card change log within 1 working day.

**Keep the folder alive.** Anything Sales learns later — new contacts, commitments made at executive level, relationship changes — goes into the folder the same week. Delivery files its own documents per SOP-DEL-02.

**11. Content Rules**

> • **Originals, not summaries.** Signed PDFs, native drawings, the client's own KPI file, exported emails.
>
> • **Nothing is entered twice.** What the client wrote in the Site Information Card is attached, not copied into the Handover Card. The card and the Handover Card never ask the client the same question.
>
> • **Professional wording in notes about people.** Facts and observed behaviour, not opinions. Write as if the person might read it.
>
> • **English is the working language of the folder.** Source documents stay in their original language; the Handover Card summarises them in English.
>
> • **Versions, naming and what must never be stored** (passwords, ID copies): SOP-DEL-02.

**12. Implementation**

**Intake Register.** The Delivery Director logs every submission: Project ID, date submitted, decision, days to acceptance, number of returns. Reviewed monthly with the Sales Lead.

**Projects already in delivery.** On the effective date, Sales completes the handover folder for every active overseas project within 10 working days, in the priority order set by the Delivery Director. Delivery work continues meanwhile; gaps found are logged as open items.

**Templates.** Delivery sets up the folder templates (SOP-DEL-02) and the Intake Register in the team space before the effective date.
