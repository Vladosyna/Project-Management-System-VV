**1. Purpose**

One fixed way to prepare, record and file a site visit, so nothing has to be reconstructed from memory and every report is comparable to every other one. Recording uses DingTalk's built-in "Action Record" minutes template — no new software, only a fixed order to what is said.

**2. Visit Types and Where Each Report Goes**

The visit type decides the folder. Folder rules and the L / S standards are defined in SOP-DEL-02.

| **Visit type**                      | **Purpose**                                                   | **Standard L**                  | **Standard S** |
|-------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|----------------|
| **Site survey**                     | Before design: measure, photograph, check network and access. | 03_Site Visits                  | 02             |
| **Technical inspection**            | Diagnose a problem or confirm conditions with the client.     | 03_Site Visits                  | 02 or 06       |
| **Installation / supervision**      | Installers on site; VionVision supervises or installs.        | 03_Site Visits                  | 06             |
| **Commissioning and accuracy test** | Configuration, calibration, accuracy test on site.            | 03_Site Visits; test data in 07 | 08             |
| **Maintenance / support**           | Any visit after the client has accepted the site.             | 09_Operation                    | 09             |

**3. Before the Visit**

> • **Read the site's Site Information Card (02) and Handover Card:** site contacts, access procedure, working window, advance-notice list (SOP-DEL-04), plus communication rules (SOP-DEL-01, items E1–E3).
>
> • **Notify security and the facility manager in advance** — who is coming, where, when. Skipping this has been the biggest single cause of wasted visits. Client contacts are messaged within their working hours only.
>
> • **Two separate emails:** resource requests to installers, access requests to the client. Never in one thread.
>
> • **Know what "complete" means for this visit:** list the points or areas to cover, so coverage can be reported as X of Y.
>
> • **Take the current documents:** latest point design and drawings, device list for installation visits, the site's Site Register row.
>
> • **New site or person names:** add them to DingTalk hot words first (Section 8).

**4. During the Visit**

> • **Track coverage as you go:** what was inspected, what was not, and why.
>
> • **Separate "agreed" from "discussed".** Note who agreed what — names, not "the client".
>
> • **Every action gets an owner and a date,** including your own.
>
> • **Photos are tied to a point number or location,** not just taken.
>
> • **No new commitments on site.** Requests for more sensors, extra work or new dates are recorded as client requests and passed to Sales and the Delivery Director — not accepted on the spot (SOP-DEL-01, Section 10).

**5. After the Visit — the Record**

**5.1 Internal record — same day, every visit**

Open a call or recording in DingTalk, choose the Action Record template, and speak the fields in this order, in short plain sentences:

| **Action Record field** | **Fill with**                                              | **Say something like**                                                                 |
|-------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Conference Theme**    | \<Project ID\> — \<Site\> — \<visit type\>                 | "NEINVER-ES-MAD-001, Las Rozas, technical inspection."                                 |
| **Date**                | Visit date                                                 | "Today, 17 September."                                                                 |
| **Attendees**           | Everyone present, marked VionVision / client / third party | "Me and Sergio from Neinver, plus the installer's site lead."                          |
| **Executive Summary**   | 2–3 sentences: what happened, why, outcome                 | "Came to check coverage after the switch upgrade. Coverage confirmed on 4 of 6 zones." |
| **Decided**             | What was actually agreed on site, and with whom            | "Agreed with Sergio that the remaining two zones are checked next visit."              |
| **Key Points**          | Factual findings. State coverage explicitly: X of Y        | "Inspected 6 of 20 points. 14 pending — no access to the top floor."                   |
| **Key Action Items**    | Owner — Task — Deadline. Say the owner's name              | "Action item: Xavier to send the fibre spec to Francesco by Friday."                   |
| **Follow-up**           | Next visit, who chases what, escalation flag               | "Next visit on the 24th. Nothing to escalate."                                         |

**5.2 Formal Site Visit Report (SVR) — when the client needs it**

> • **When:** Standard L surveys and inspections whose findings change design, scope or dates, and any visit the client asks a written report for.
>
> • **What:** the same fields as 5.1, written up in the house template, in English. Document code SVR-NN, a running number per project.
>
> • **When by:** within 2 working days of the visit.
>
> • **Review:** the Delivery Director reviews it before it goes to the client. The PM sends it to the client contact, with the CC rules from the Handover Card (item E3).

**6. Filing**

**File names, per SOP-DEL-02:** internal export **YYYY-MM-DD_Site-Visit\_\<Site\>\_vN.pdf**; formal report **PROJECT-ID_SVR-NN_Site-Visit-Report.pdf** (e.g. NEINVER-DE-LEJ-001_SVR-01_Site-Visit-Report.pdf). Standard S files start with their slot number, e.g. 06_Site-Visit_2026-10-02.pdf.

| **Record**                                   | **Standard L**                                                     | **Standard S**                                              |
|----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Internal record (DingTalk Action Record)** | Stays in DingTalk, tagged "Site visit". PDF export filed as below. | Same                                                        |
| **Visit report — PDF export or formal SVR**  | 03_Site Visits (after acceptance: 09_Operation)                    | 02 (survey) · 06 (installation) · 09 (after acceptance)     |
| **Photos**                                   | Subfolder YYYY-MM-DD_Photos next to the report                     | Inside the report PDF, or one zip with the same slot prefix |
| **Accuracy test data from the visit**        | 07_Commissioning & Testing                                         | 08                                                          |
| **Action items**                             | Issue log in 10_Project Management                                 | Site Register — Notes column                                |
| **Installation or acceptance date**          | Site Register, same day                                            | Site Register, same day                                     |
| **Scope or date change agreed on site**      | Handover Card change log + 01_Commercial (Sales informed)          | Handover Card change log + 01 (Sales informed)              |

**7. Escalation**

If something needs escalating, do not rely on anyone finding it in the note. Flag it directly, the same day: first the Delivery Director (Vladyslav), then Leon Zhao if it needs to go further. Hai Tao only for something genuinely out of the ordinary. If the client is waiting for an answer, send a short holding reply the same day, cleared with whoever the issue was escalated to.

**8. Known DingTalk Limitations**

| **Limitation**                                                                                        | **What to do**                                                                                 |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Auto-extracted action items do not reliably carry an owner (assignee came back blank on a real call). | Say the owner's name out loud for every action item and check the table by hand before filing. |
| A note cannot be tagged automatically.                                                                | Tag it "Site visit" in the DingTalk app — one tap after dictation.                             |
| Site and person names get mistranscribed.                                                             | Add new site, client and contact names to DingTalk hot words before the visit.                 |

**9. If a Visit Doesn't Fit**

Use the template anyway, as far as it goes, and add a plain-language note at the end for anything without a natural field. A slightly awkward fit beats no record.
