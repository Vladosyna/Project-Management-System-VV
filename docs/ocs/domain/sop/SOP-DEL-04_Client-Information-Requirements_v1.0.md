**1. Purpose and the Rule**

SOP-DEL-01 defines what Sales hands to Delivery. This SOP runs the other way: Delivery defines exactly what it needs from the client, packages it so the client can answer once, and Sales collects it while it still owns the relationship. The returned card becomes the technical core of the handover package.

**4. Rules for the Card**

> • **No passwords or logins in the card.** Switch, NVR and platform credentials are exchanged directly with the PM by a separate channel, never written into the card or its attachments.
>
> • **Business contact details only.** No personal data beyond name, role, work email and phone.
>
> • **The client's own documents win.** If the client has its own KPI list, drawings or IT requirements, they are attached as they are; the card points to them rather than paraphrasing.
>
> • **English.** A bilingual version can be issued for clients whose site teams need it.
>
> • **Changes after return** (new opening date, network change, extra entrance) are sent by the client as an updated card, version number raised, old one to \_Archive.
